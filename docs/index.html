Strategy rules
  1. Weekly CHoCH: the weekly high breaks the last swing high while the structure is bearish (BigBeluga “Choch Pattern Levels”, length 10).
  2. No entry on the CHoCH. Wait for a pullback: the previous week closed above the 20 or 50 EMA, this week’s low touches it (within 1%) and the week closes back above it.
  3. Entry at that weekly close. Stop below the support candle’s low. Target 2R, or exit on a bearish CHoCH.
  4. The setup is cancelled if price closes below the CHoCH level, a bearish CHoCH comes first, or no pullback happens within 26 weeks.
